## Critical Reflections: Key Themes from this Portfolio
### 1. Copilot and AI-Assisted Coding
- Copilot accelerated my learning, particularly in writing boilerplate code and exploring unfamiliar libraries.
- However, Copilot sometimes provided incomplete or incorrect suggestions, requiring critical evaluation.
- This dual experience highlighted the importance of **human oversight**, particularly in creative or culturally sensitive contexts.

### 2. AI and Cultural Bias
- Machine learning models are often trained on **Western-dominated datasets**, leading to underrepresentation and misinterpretation of non-Western cultures.
- Through the **Creative LLM Design Notebooks**, I critically examined how these biases manifest and why **cultural diversity in training data** is crucial for fair and inclusive AI systems.

### 3. Ethical and Social Questions
- Who is the “author” of AI-generated content? This question underpinned the **Creative Design Notebook**, which explores **co-authorship between humans and AI**.
- In cultural analysis projects, how do we ensure that **historically marginalized voices are represented**, rather than erased or distorted by algorithmic shortcuts?
- These ethical concerns connect directly to ongoing debates in **digital humanities** about power, authority, and the role of computation in shaping historical narratives.
